# Sales_Analytics_Dashboard
Sales Analytics Dashboard

A comprehensive business intelligence solution that transforms raw sales data into actionable insights through automated data pipelines and interactive dashboards.

🎯 Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end data analytics workflow: data cleaning → database design → BI visualization. The dashboard tracks over 1,000 orders and $5M in revenue, enabling data-driven decisions across sales teams and regional management.

Key Metrics:


1,000+ total orders analyzed
25K units sold across product categories
$5M total revenue tracked
51.19% profit margin visibility
4 regions and 3 sales channels analyzed



📊 Dashboard Features

KPI Overview


Total Orders: 1,000+
Total Quantity Sold: 25K units
Total Profit: $3M
Total Revenue: $5M
Profit Margin: 51.19%


Interactive Dimensions


By Region: North, East, West, South performance comparison
By Product Category: Clothing, Electronics, Furniture, Home & Garden sales breakdown
By Sales Channel: Retail, Online, and direct sales mix
By Sales Representative: Individual performance tracking across teams
By Payment Method: Credit Card, Bank Transfer, Cash adoption rates


Time-Based Analysis


Monthly Trend Line: 12-month sales trajectory with seasonality insights
Quarterly Breakdown: Seasonal patterns and revenue peaks
